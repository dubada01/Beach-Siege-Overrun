Privacy Policy for Beach Siege: Overrun Last updated: November 7, 2026 Beach Siege: Overrun is operated by DubaTech Studio. This policy explains how the game handles player information, gameplay analytics, leaderboards and in-app purchases. Privacy questions and deletion requests Email dubatechstudio@gmail.com with the subject "Beach Siege data request" and state whether you want access to or deletion of your data. In Android version 3.3 build 26 and later, eligible online players can open Settings, tap "Copy Privacy Request Details", and paste those details into the email. Copy the details before turning Gameplay Analytics off, which removes its identifier from your device. On older versions, include your leaderboard name and the record you want removed so we can help identify it. Never send a password or payment-card details. See Requests and deletion below for what is removed and what providers may retain. Age settings and children Starting with Android version 3.3 build 26, the game asks your age once to choose privacy settings. It stores only an under-13 or 13-and-over band on your device; your exact age is neither stored nor transmitted. Players under 13, and players who have not answered, use local personal best scores. The game does not request a public leaderboard name, connect them to Unity leaderboards, initialize Google Play Games or Game Center, or collect first-party gameplay analytics. Scores and progress stay on the device, subject to the device's backup settings. Native store checkout and its purchase records remain governed by the platform account and parental purchase-approval settings. The age restriction is not a parental consent flow for online services. Earlier releases, including earlier iOS builds, do not have this age gate. If a child used online services in an earlier release, contact us to request deletion of records controlled by DubaTech Studio. Game progress and device storage The game stores progress, settings, Pearl and item balances, unlocked powers, and purchase-delivery records on your device. These records keep your progress and help prevent a purchase from being granted twice. Local records remain with the app's saved data until that data is cleared. Your device or platform backup settings may retain copies. Leaderboards and platform game services Online leaderboards use a player identifier, your chosen display name, scores and relevant gameplay results, such as faction, rank and run time. Names, scores and ranking details may be visible to other players. Unity Authentication creates a pseudonymous player identity for Unity Leaderboards. Supported platform features also use Apple Game Center or Google Play Games identities, achievements and scores. These services use this information to authenticate players, publish rankings, record achievements and operate the game services. Unity services also receive IP addresses and may derive approximate location from network information. Platform game-service SDKs collect usage analytics and technical diagnostics for service stability and product improvements. The game does not request precise device location. Online ranking records are retained to maintain leaderboards. Contact us to request deletion of records controlled by DubaTech Studio. Apple, Google and Unity also provide privacy controls for data they control. Gameplay analytics DubaTech Studio operates gameplay analytics at orbs-stats.com to improve onboarding, progression, game balance and app quality. When Gameplay Analytics is enabled, the game sends a random installation identifier, random session and trial identifiers, event times, app version, platform, tutorial activity and gameplay results. Results include trial mode, faction, difficulty, wave, rank, duration, remaining lives, Pearl balance at session start, faction unlocks and tower-level milestones. These events are linked to the random installation identifier. That identifier is separate from your leaderboard identity. This analytics feature excludes player names, email addresses, advertising identifiers, precise location, purchase receipts and payment details. Network infrastructure receives an IP address during delivery. The analytics database does not store IP addresses. Hosting providers may process network information and request logs to deliver and secure the service. Analytics choice and retention Turn Gameplay Analytics off in the game's Settings to stop new collection, clear unsent events and remove the local analytics identifier. Turning it back on creates a new identifier. Data already delivered follows the retention schedule below. Unsent events are stored for up to 14 days, with a limit of 512 events. Server event records have a 90-day retention limit, with cleanup every six hours. Installation activity records expire after 90 days of inactivity. Further technical details are available at https://orbs-stats.com/beach-siege/privacy.html In-app purchases Google Play or Apple's App Store processes checkout and payment using your store account. The game uses Unity In-App Purchasing to communicate with the platform store. The game processes product identifiers, transaction identifiers, purchase receipts or tokens, purchase status and entitlement information to verify purchases, deliver Pearls, gameplay items and powers, restore eligible purchases and handle refunds or revocations. Purchase-delivery records are stored with the game's local save. The store maintains transaction records under its own policies. DubaTech's gameplay analytics service does not receive purchase receipts. Payment-card details are handled by the store and are not provided to the game. Clearing local data can remove consumable balances. Eligible permanent purchases can be restored through the same platform account. Service providers and data sharing We use Unity for authentication and leaderboards, Apple and Google for platform game services and store purchases, and hosting providers for our gameplay analytics service. Information needed for these features is processed by the relevant providers. Providers may also process network information, technical identifiers and service diagnostics under their own policies. We do not sell or rent player information. Public leaderboard entries are visible to other players as described above. Provider privacy information is available at https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ https://policies.google.com/privacy Security Our gameplay analytics uploads use HTTPS. Access to the analytics dashboard is restricted to the operator. We use reasonable measures to protect information handled by the game and its services. Requests and deletion You can request access to or deletion of information controlled by DubaTech Studio at dubatechstudio@gmail.com. We may need information to locate the record and verify that it belongs to you. Because gameplay analytics uses a random installation identifier, a leaderboard name or email alone may not identify those records. Records that cannot be linked to a request continue to expire under the analytics retention schedule. For verified deletion requests we remove the matching first-party analytics events and installation activity, and applicable Unity leaderboard entries and authentication records controlled by us. We may retain the correspondence needed to handle the request. Online scores are otherwise kept until deleted or the leaderboard is reset; first-party analytics follows the 90-day retention periods above. Clearing device data removes local game records, subject to your platform's backup behavior. Contact the relevant provider for store-account or platform-service records it controls. Transaction information may be retained by the store for payment, fraud prevention and legal obligations. Children's privacy If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact dubatechstudio@gmail.com so we can review and address the request.
