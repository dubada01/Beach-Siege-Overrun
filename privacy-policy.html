Privacy Policy for Beach Siege: Overrun Last updated: November 16, 2026 Beach Siege: Overrun is operated by DubaTech Studio. This policy explains how the game handles player information, gameplay analytics, leaderboards, account recovery, saved wallets and in-app purchases. Privacy questions and deletion requests Email dubatechstudio@gmail.com with the subject "Beach Siege data request" and state whether you want access to or deletion of your data. In Android version 3.3 build 26 and later, eligible online players can open Settings, tap "Copy Privacy Request Details", and paste those details into the email. Copy the details before turning Gameplay Analytics off, which removes its identifier from your device. On older versions, include your leaderboard name and the record you want removed so we can help identify it. Never send a password or payment-card details. See Requests and deletion below for what is removed and what providers may retain. Age settings and children Starting with Android version 3.3 build 26, the game asks your age once to choose privacy settings. It stores only an under-13 or 13-and-over band on your device; your exact age is neither stored nor transmitted. Players under 13, and players who have not answered, use local personal best scores. The game does not request a public leaderboard name, connect them to Unity leaderboards, initialize Google Play Games or Game Center, or collect first-party gameplay analytics. Scores and progress stay on the device, subject to the device's backup settings. In account-enabled releases, new purchases require a linked online game account and are unavailable to players who cannot use online services. Platform store records and parental purchase-approval settings remain subject to Apple or Google's policies. The age restriction is not a parental consent flow for online services. Earlier releases, including earlier iOS builds, do not have this age gate. If a child used online services in an earlier release, contact us to request deletion of records controlled by DubaTech Studio. Game progress and device storage The game stores progress, settings, Pearl and item balances, unlocked powers, and purchase-delivery records on your device. These records keep your progress and help prevent a purchase from being granted twice. Local records remain with the app's saved data until that data is cleared. Your device or platform backup settings may retain copies. Accounts, cloud wallets and recovery In account-enabled releases, eligible players can choose Sign in with Apple on iOS or Google Play Games on Android. Unity Authentication links the platform identity to a game player ID. This feature does not request your platform password, name or email address. Our account service at orbs-stats.com stores the player ID, Pearl and item balances, purchased powers, unlocks, tower levels and experience, contracts, personal bests and other saved game progress. We process these records to operate the game, sync your save, recover progress and deliver and protect purchases. Account data is separate from optional gameplay analytics and remains necessary while using a cloud account even if Gameplay Analytics is turned off. The first migration imports the available device save once and keeps a device backup. Later recovery uses the remaining saved balance, without refilling already-spent supplies. Offline changes remain on the device until synced and cannot be recovered elsewhere before upload. Use the same platform sign-in to recover your account. Linking Apple and Android identities to one shared account is not currently available. Store test purchases use a separate test wallet; test credits do not become real-money wallet credits. Cloud accounts remain until deletion is requested. The service keeps fourteen daily backup copies on the hosting server and up to thirty dated encrypted daily copies on a separate home server. Copies expire as new successful backups replace them. Backups are restricted to disaster recovery, and deletion records must be reapplied after a restore. Leaderboards and platform game services Online leaderboards use a player identifier, your chosen display name, scores and relevant gameplay results, such as faction, rank and run time. Names, scores and ranking details may be visible to other players. Unity Authentication creates a pseudonymous player identity for Unity Leaderboards. Supported platform features also use Apple Game Center or Google Play Games identities, achievements and scores. These services use this information to authenticate players, publish rankings, record achievements and operate the game services. Unity services also receive IP addresses and may derive approximate location from network information. Platform game-service SDKs collect usage analytics and technical diagnostics for service stability and product improvements. The game does not request precise device location. Online ranking records are retained to maintain leaderboards. Contact us to request deletion of records controlled by DubaTech Studio. Apple, Google and Unity also provide privacy controls for data they control. Gameplay analytics DubaTech Studio operates gameplay analytics at orbs-stats.com to improve onboarding, progression, game balance and app quality. When Gameplay Analytics is enabled, the game sends a random installation identifier, random session and trial identifiers, event times, app version, platform, tutorial activity and gameplay results. Results include trial mode, faction, difficulty, wave, rank, duration, remaining lives, Pearl balance at session start, faction unlocks and tower-level milestones. These events are linked to the random installation identifier. That identifier is separate from your leaderboard identity. This analytics feature excludes player names, email addresses, advertising identifiers, precise location, purchase receipts and payment details. Network infrastructure receives an IP address during delivery. The analytics database does not store IP addresses. Hosting providers may process network information and request logs to deliver and secure the service. Analytics choice and retention Turn Gameplay Analytics off in the game's Settings to stop new collection, clear unsent events and remove the local analytics identifier. Turning it back on creates a new identifier. Data already delivered follows the retention schedule below. Unsent events are stored for up to 14 days, with a limit of 512 events. Server event records have a 90-day retention limit, with cleanup every six hours. Installation activity records expire after 90 days of inactivity. Further technical details are available at https://orbs-stats.com/beach-siege/privacy.html In-app purchases Google Play or Apple's App Store processes checkout and payment using your store account. The game uses Unity In-App Purchasing to communicate with the platform store. The game processes product identifiers, transaction identifiers, purchase receipts or tokens, purchase status and entitlement information to verify purchases, deliver Pearls, gameplay items and powers, restore eligible purchases and handle refunds or revocations. In account-enabled releases, the server checks purchases with Apple or Google and stores transaction IDs, product IDs, delivered rewards and encrypted purchase proofs to recheck refunds and revocations. Local purchase-delivery records also remain with the game's save. Older releases may keep delivery records only on the device. The store retains its own transaction records. DubaTech's gameplay analytics service does not receive purchase receipts. Payment-card details are handled by the store and are not provided to the game. Unsynced or guest consumable balances can be lost when local data is cleared. Linked accounts recover the remaining synced balance through the same game sign-in. Eligible permanent purchases can be restored through the same platform store account, subject to game-account ownership. Service providers and data sharing We use Unity for authentication and leaderboards, Apple and Google for platform game services and store purchases, and hosting providers for our gameplay analytics and account wallet services. Information needed for these features is processed by the relevant providers. Providers may also process network information, technical identifiers and service diagnostics under their own policies. We do not sell or rent player information. Public leaderboard entries are visible to other players as described above. Provider privacy information is available at https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ https://policies.google.com/privacy Security Our gameplay analytics and account uploads use HTTPS. Operator access to the dashboard and wallet service is restricted. Purchase proofs and independent backup copies are encrypted. We use reasonable measures to protect information handled by the game and its services. Requests and deletion You can request access to or deletion of information controlled by DubaTech Studio at dubatechstudio@gmail.com. We may need information to locate the record and verify that it belongs to you. Because gameplay analytics uses a random installation identifier, a leaderboard name or email alone may not identify those records. Records that cannot be linked to a request continue to expire under the analytics retention schedule. For verified deletion requests we remove the matching first-party analytics events and installation activity, and applicable Unity leaderboard entries and authentication records controlled by us. We may retain the correspondence needed to handle the request. Online scores are otherwise kept until deleted or the leaderboard is reset; first-party analytics follows the 90-day retention periods above. In version 3.4 (iOS build 9 / Android build 29) and later, open Settings > Your account > Manage account > Delete account. Confirmation and a fresh platform sign-in are required. Once accepted, server cleanup continues automatically even if you close the app. It removes the Unity game identity, both live and test cloud wallets, progress, operation logs, encrypted purchase proofs and Unity leaderboard entries. The app clears game progress and account backups on that device after acceptance. If the connection is interrupted, choose Finish deletion when connected to confirm the request. Use the email request above for older versions or other remaining records; we may need your player ID to locate them. You can also request account and associated data deletion without reinstalling the app at https://orbs-stats.com/beach-siege-wallet/delete-account.html Deletion does not refund purchases or delete your Apple or Google account. Minimal transaction, migration and deletion records may remain to prevent duplicate purchase credits, abuse and restoration of deleted accounts. Restricted disaster-recovery backups expire under the backup schedule above and are not used to reinstate deleted accounts. Optional analytics uses a separate installation ID; include the privacy request details to identify and delete those records as well. Clearing device data removes local game records, subject to your platform's backup behavior. Contact the relevant provider for store-account or platform-service records it controls. Transaction information may be retained by the store for payment, fraud prevention and legal obligations. Children's privacy If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact dubatechstudio@gmail.com so we can review and address the request.
